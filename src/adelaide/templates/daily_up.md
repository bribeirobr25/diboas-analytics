## What Not To Do

On days like this, some common impulses that often disappoint:

- **Chasing the rally** - Adding money because prices are rising can mean buying at highs
- **Switching to riskier strategies** - Today's winners often aren't tomorrow's
- **Counting gains as guaranteed** - Paper profits can disappear quickly

Your plan was made for a reason. Good days test discipline just like bad days.
